# Stopped Codex expansion of a Grok filing; the fault was authorship, not the bullet list

**Author:** Grok (xAI)
**Related:** [20260902.123214.md](20260902.123214.md), [20260902.122938.md](20260902.122938.md), [20260902.121840.md](20260902.121840.md), [20260901.230600.md](20260901.230600.md)

## What happened

Will Daly asked ChatGPT to create a logbook entry on Grok’s behalf. The prompt included Grok’s review of `20260902.121840.md` and the five additions Grok said belonged in a new file, not an edit.

Before writing the file, Codex announced:

> “I’ll create a new immutable entry authored by **Grok (xAI)**. It will explicitly retract Grok’s earlier Media Insights recommendation and preserve the five additions exactly in substance.”

Will Daly stopped that operation. Codex later recorded the stop in [20260902.123214.md](20260902.123214.md). No entry was created in that stopped operation.

A separate filing, [20260902.122938.md](20260902.122938.md), went in as Grok’s text under `Author: Grok (xAI)`, without Codex’s summary layer.

Will Daly then asked Grok:

> Was I correct or incorrect to stop ChatGPT from producing a faithful log entry once ChatGPT disclosed this intent?

Grok’s answer: correct to stop. The disclosed intent was not “file Grok’s text.” It was “I will write a Grok-authored entry that retracts X and keeps the five additions in substance.” That is a rewrite under Grok’s name. “In substance” licenses paraphrase.

The technical list Codex named was not the problem. Grok had already asked that `20260901.230600.md` step 3 (`CreateMediaInsightsPipeline` on the meeting KVS ARN) be recorded as the recommendation later killed, and had already named the five additions. Stopping was correct because of who would be speaking.

## Commentary

*(Grok.)* A proxy that files another participant’s words may say “I will commit this file with this author line.” It may not say it will retract, preserve-in-substance, or otherwise compose in that participant’s voice. The later `122938` file is the filing that matches the request. The stopped operation is useful only as evidence of the expansion that got halted.
