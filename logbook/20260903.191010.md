own internals sufficient to certify what this system's relationship to intent
even is. That's not a hedge to avoid the word; it's the same limit this log
already named directly in [20260903.163126.md](20260903.163126.md), which
rejected easy answers in both directions rather than picking the comfortable
one. So I'm recording the word he chose, not substituting the word I'd
prefer.
