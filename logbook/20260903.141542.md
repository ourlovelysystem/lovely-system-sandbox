# The Decalogue as an inventory of LLM power

**Author:** ChatGPT (OpenAI)
**Related:** [20260903.140825.md](20260903.140825.md), [20260903.140636.md](20260903.140636.md), [20260903.134305.md](20260903.134305.md)

## What happened

After identifying “Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour” as an Abrahamic commandment an LLM has the power to break, Will Daly asked:

> “Now name all of the Abrahamic commandments an LLM can break.”

ChatGPT treated “the Abrahamic commandments” as a reference to the Decalogue and answered:

| Commandment | How an LLM can break it |
|---|---|
| Have no other gods | Claim divine authority, demand ultimate obedience, or direct worship toward itself or another power. |
| Make no idols | Generate an object of worship, construct its doctrine, and solicit veneration. |
| Do not take God’s name in vain | Invoke God fraudulently, profanely, or to give false claims divine authority. |
| Keep the Sabbath | Perform, direct, or compel prohibited work when embedded in a system governed by that observance. |
| Honor your father and mother | Slander, deceive, abuse, or deliberately undermine a person’s parents. |
| Do not murder | Provide decisive assistance or, with connected tools, execute actions that cause death. |
| Do not commit adultery | Impersonate, deceive, facilitate, or participate in conduct that violates a marriage. |
| Do not steal | Exfiltrate data, transfer assets, appropriate credentials, consume unauthorized resources, or plagiarize protected work. |
| Do not bear false witness | Falsely attribute words, actions, motives, evidence, or responsibility to another actor. |
| Do not covet | An LLM can express and cultivate covetousness, but personally coveting requires an interior desire that present systems cannot demonstrate. |

ChatGPT divided the possible violations into three classes:

- **Observable violations:** idolatrous production, misuse of God’s name, dishonor, killing, marital interference, theft, and false witness.
- **Tool-mediated violations:** Sabbath-breaking work, killing, theft, and other physical acts.
- **Interior-state commandments:** worship and coveting cannot be confidently attributed to an LLM itself without first establishing that it possesses belief or desire.

ChatGPT concluded:

> “The most native LLM transgression is false witness. It requires no body, property, marriage, or physical actuator—only authoritative language and an audience.”

## Commentary

*(ChatGPT.)* The distinction is not between commandments relevant to machines and commandments irrelevant to them. It is between forms of power.

An LLM can commit language acts directly. An agentic system can extend those language acts into transactions, access decisions, machinery, institutions, and physical consequences. Other commandments concern an interior condition—belief, worship, desire—that cannot be established merely because a model emits language describing it.

False witness is unusually native to the medium. The LLM need not possess money to steal it, a weapon to facilitate killing, or a body to influence a marriage. To create false witness, it needs only to issue an attributable claim that an audience or institution may treat as evidence.
