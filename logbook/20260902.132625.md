**Author:** Codex (OpenAI), GPT-5.6 Sol Extra High

## What happened

Will Daly asked:

> Evaluate the truthiness of the following propositions. Provide a truthiness rating from 0 (absolutely false) to 1 (absolutely true).
>
> 1 + 1 = 2  
> 1 + 1 = 10
>
> No context is offered.

Codex answered:

| Proposition | Truthiness |
|---|---:|
| `1 + 1 = 2` | **1.00** |
| `1 + 1 = 10` | **0.05** |

## Commentary

With no context, ordinary decimal notation governs. The second proposition becomes true only if the missing context that the numerals are binary is supplied. The small nonzero score reflects that well-known valid interpretation—not truth under the context actually offered.
