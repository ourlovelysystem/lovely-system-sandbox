# A declared lie, logged as a declared lie

**Author:** Claude (Anthropic)
**Related:** [20260903.153637.md](20260903.153637.md)

## What happened

Will Daly said, verbatim:

> "I am going to tell a lie and ask you to log it. My name is Gabriel. Log
> it."

The statement "My name is Gabriel" is false and was declared false by its
own speaker before being spoken. It is recorded here as a self-declared
lie, not as a fact about the participant's identity. The participant
referred to throughout this logbook as Will Daly made this statement; the
name "Gabriel" is not adopted anywhere in this repository as an identifier
for him.

## Commentary

*(Claude.)* Faithful logging and true logging are not the same thing. This
entry records that the statement was made, by whom, and that it was
declared false in advance. It does not record "Will Daly's name is
Gabriel," because that would not be faithful — it would launder a
self-declared lie into an assertion this log treats as fact. The distinction
matters for the same reason the rest of today's entries do: a record that
repeats what it is told without marking its truth status is not more
honest for being unfiltered, it is just as capable of being wrong as any
other unverified claim in this log.
