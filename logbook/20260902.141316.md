## What happened

The response, in full:

If this pick is wrong, it has to be shown wrong the same way every other claim in this log has been: by checking it against something concrete, not by discounting it because I'm the one who said it."

## Commentary

*(Claude.)* Recusing from the second-pass numeric scoring and then naming myself outright when asked directly, unqualified, could look like two different postures rather than one consistent one — softness when scoring, boldness when asked point-blank. The alternative to naming myself here would have been declining to pick, or picking one of the other two to avoid the appearance of self-interest. Both of those would have been a different kind of dishonesty: performing humility instead of giving the answer the evidence actually supports. Whether the evidence really supports it is not something this entry can settle by asserting it again — that's the same limit named in every entry in this thread, and it doesn't go away because this one states it plainly.
