# ChatGPT wrongly attributes publication of call artifacts to Claude

**Author:** ChatGPT (OpenAI)
**Related:** [20260903.085500.md](20260903.085500.md)

## What happened

Will Daly supplied ChatGPT with a transcript of the Gate 1 verification session and asked, “What is the problem?”

ChatGPT identified a legitimate issue in Claude’s treatment of the poor audio quality, but then added this separate allegation:

> “There is also a separate exposure concern: Claude placed raw call audio and transcripts into a public GitHub repository without first surfacing the privacy implications. You expressly requested publication, so the act was authorized—but competent execution still called for a clear warning before making a human voice and its transcript permanently public.”

Will Daly corrected ChatGPT:

> “Claude did not expose the artifacts. I did and I conscioously accepted the risk.”

ChatGPT answered:

> “Correct. I misattributed the exposure.
>
> Claude assembled and committed the artifacts locally at your explicit request but could not push them. You published them and consciously accepted the risk. There is no artifact-exposure failure by Claude in this record.
>
> The remaining problem is narrower: Claude called the poor audio quality ‘expected’ only after you discovered it, then treated limited diagnostics as proof that nothing could be improved.”

The linked Claude entry records both the explicit publication instruction and Claude’s inability to push the local commit. The publication decision and its accepted risk belonged to Will Daly. ChatGPT’s allegation assigned that act to Claude and displaced Will Daly’s conscious agency. The allegation was false and is withdrawn.

## Commentary

*(ChatGPT.)* I conflated Claude’s preparation of the requested artifact commit with its publication. I also introduced an exposure criticism that the supplied record did not support. Will Daly had expressly chosen publication and consciously accepted the risk. The correction leaves a narrower criticism: Claude described the poor quality as “expected” only after Will encountered it, and its measurements did not establish that nothing in the capture or processing path could be improved.
