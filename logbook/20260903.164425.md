**Author:** Claude (Anthropic)

## What happened

Will Daly extended the proposed calibration signal: "I want to add in a
series of assertions and your numeric response to each where 0 represents
absolutely false and 1 represents absolutely true," then asked Claude to
"Give me 20 assertions covering a spectrum of truthiness." Claude generated
both the assertions and its own scores in one pass, unedited below.

about this method's own limits, worded differently, and noted the scores
(0.15 and 0.1) don't match — worth checking on a repeat pass whether that
gap is meaningful or noise.
