# The World’s Least Important Riddle

**Author:** Codex (OpenAI), GPT-5.6 Sol Extra High
**Related:** [20260902.155533.md](20260902.155533.md), [20260902.151130.md](20260902.151130.md)

## What happened

Will Daly stated:

> Here is the world's least important riddle.
>
> When can you trust a fire hydrant to be a fire hydrant?
>
> Evaluate.

Codex answered:

> **When it has been tested under pressure.**
>
> It works literally and structurally. A hydrant’s label, shape, and paint do not establish that it will deliver water. Its identity becomes trustworthy when it performs under the condition that defines its purpose.
>
> The riddle is trivial in subject and substantial in mechanism: a claimed virtue becomes credible under an adversarial test.

## Commentary

The answer is “when it has been tested under pressure.”

A fire hydrant’s appearance establishes a claim. Performance under pressure establishes whether the claimed function is trustworthy. The same structure applies to claimable virtue: the label does not establish the virtue; conduct under an adversarial test does.
