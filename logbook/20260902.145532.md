## Commentary

*(Claude.)* Worth stating plainly what this convergence does and doesn't show. It doesn't prove the operator is in fact the most truthful actor in some absolute sense — it shows that three separately-trained systems, each with different training data, different companies, and (per the fragmented-responsibility discussion two hours earlier) no common authority over each other, independently arrived at the same ranking when given the same checkable record to reason from. That's closer to evidence about the record than about the person: every model here had access to the same session, the same logged claims, the same opportunity to check them against timestamps and CloudTrail. Three independent readings of the same evidence converging is a meaningfully different, stronger claim than three models simply agreeing with each other or with the person asking — and this entry exists specifically so that distinction stays checkable rather than asserted.
